## Failure and correction

The daily job was running successfully. The November20 CGIL general declaration had no transport keywords, so `isTransportRelevantRow` dropped it before normalization. A separate CUB/SGB November20–21 rail declaration existed but was hidden as UNKNOWN geography: a TPL-only company list containing Udine contaminated the whole announcement's geography. Its co-headed `FERROVIARIO, TRASPORTO MERCI SU ROTAIA` phrase left an empty rail section, losing the23-hour interval. The dated TPL shift was also duplicated over both days.

The deterministic correction retains a general declaration as **pending transport applicability**, without turning a workday into numeric hours or inventing operators/lines/guarantees. Additive `timing_evidence.fields.modeApplicability` distinguishes `GENERAL_NOTICE_PENDING` from `EXPLICIT_TRANSPORT`. Metro candidates project only to the existing metro cities. A later matching official operator notice can promote the applicable local candidate; other cities stay pending. Explicit whole-transport and per-sector exclusions win, including October30 CIB-UNICOBAS/USI-CIT/ADL notices. Their absence from passenger cards is deliberate.

Co-headed sectors share their declared interval; independent rail/contractor/other-mode clauses remain separate. An explicit shift date sets the event day without supplying clock hours. November20 rail now represents21:00–24:00 and November21 represents00:00–20:00. Only the November21 TPL clause projects to supported areas of its listed companies: Torino/Napoli/Bari; `operatorRestriction` preserves the complete original company list. This is not a promise that all GTT/EAV services stop, or that the official scope is limited to these three supported cities. Exact lines and shift clocks remain unknown. Its TPL locality list no longer narrows national rail geography.

Current-list checks also found national Techno Sky incorrectly UNKNOWN and two Bari road-division notices incorrectly spawning train events because the employer's name contained Ferrovie. Their national aviation workforce / bus division are now distinguished, without generating airport-wide affected-line lists. Official Techno Sky context: [ENAV](https://www.enav.it/node/18604); strike facts remain [MIT](https://scioperi.mit.gov.it/mit2/public/scioperi).
