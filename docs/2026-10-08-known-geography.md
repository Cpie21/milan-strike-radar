# Known administrative geography versus page coverage

The production Foggia row retained MIT's Puglia/Foggia declaration and `UNSUPPORTED_CITY`, but `region=UNKNOWN`. Udine and two Start Romagna announcements had the same mismatch. It was a backend modeling boundary: `classifyRegionTags` only knew the20 public page cities. Official geography had not been lost, but the standard filter field still conflated known geography with page availability.

`lib/officialGeography.ts` now canonicalizes the **structured MIT administrative declarations**, independently of the page registry. Exact province spelling/punctuation produces stable tags: Foggia→FOGGIA, Udine→UDINE, Forli'-Cesena/Forlì–Cesena→FORLI_CESENA. This is identity normalization of an official field, not a claim that a province is only its capital, a municipality/airport registry, or provider-name semantic inference. Empty/pending/unknown/national/all-placeholders never become concrete provinces. A declared regional/Tutte area without a supported projection can retain a REGION_* identifier without inventing cities.

The existing supported-city, explicit-airport, office-name, national-entity and exclusion fences remain. Unsupported locality in a supported administrative region never expands into the nearest supported city. `normalizedGeography` is additive under existing `timing_evidence.fields`, with MIT source/method/excerpt; `officialGeography` retains exact originals, `supportedCityProjection=[]` and `locationStatus=UNSUPPORTED_CITY` still state the product coverage limit. No schema migration; region stays a string. Existing20-city pages, calendar and widget selection and Ask unsupported-place fence remain. `/api/strikes?region=FOGGIA` still returns explicit unsupported-city400, not a misleading empty success or Milan answer. Enabling more public cities is separate product work.

Fresh HTML ingestion and reprocessing previously persisted UNKNOWN raw rows use the same normalization. Dates, status, modes, windows and official source_key do not change. The daily cron still refreshes and reconciles them; there is no AI/model stage or additional paid geographic lookup. Existing follow-up adapters for ATAF Foggia and Arriva Udine are retained. This correction does not invent operational details or a specific service area for a multi-basin provider.

Verification:331 regressions, TypeScript and focused ESLint passed. Named tests cover6 independent unsupported areas, every supported-city exclusion, replay identity/windows, punctuation, genuine unknown placeholders, national-airport non-expansion and unsupported regional declarations. `scripts/audit-geography-normalization.cjs` compares the live official transport list to the previous classifier, asserting every already-supported projection remains unchanged. Deployment and normal-sync receipts follow below.

Official event source: [MIT list](https://scioperi.mit.gov.it/mit2/public/scioperi), updated October8. The Foggia declaration is October15, TPL,24h, Puglia/Foggia; Udine is October20,15:00–24:00. Keep original raw data and event identity through the repair.
